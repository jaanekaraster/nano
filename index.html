Toggle layers from the sidebar. The pincode activity layer shows a 10×10 waffle at each pincode centroid; each square represents one percent of that pincode's recorded activity.

For layers labeled "pmtiles", zoom in to the urban scale to see detail.

Hover a pincode to see its category totals and percentages. The sidebar legend keeps category colors consistent between the map and the detail table.
